# Plush Alley — Who We Are & What We Sell

> Brand visuals and color themes live in [themes.md](./themes.md). This file covers the business, products, and messaging.

## 1. What is Plush Alley?

**Plush Alley** (also styled *Plush Alley & Co.*) is a **Japan-based anime merchandise shop** run out of **Taguig City, Metro Manila, Philippines**. We bring official anime plushies, figures, and other Japan-sourced goods to Filipino fans and **ship nationwide**.

- **Tagline (email footer / business card):** "Japan Based Anime Merchandise"
- **Facebook cover line:** "Hi there こんにちは! Welcome to Plush Alley — Taguig based · We ship nationwide!"
- **Facebook intro:** "Hello! Welcome to Plush Alley — we sell anime merch, figures, plushies, Japan-based goods and etc."
- **Website welcome:** "Discover a world of delightful plush toys and accessories. From cuddly companions to unique collectibles, our curated selection awaits you. Start your plush adventure today! 🧸🛒"

Mascot: a round, winking **white cat** with pink outline and lavender stripes, whose tail forms the swoosh under the wordmark. Paw prints are the secondary motif.

## 2. What we sell

## 4. Audience

- Filipino anime fans, mostly 16–35, collectors and gift buyers.
- Shoppers who want **authentic Japan-sourced** items without importing themselves.
- Marketplace shoppers (Shopee/Lazada) who discover us by character name.

## 5. Brand voice

- **Cute, warm, friendly** — like the winking cat. Light emoji use (🧸🐾💗).
- **Bilingual touches**: English with a little Japanese (こんにちは, arigatou) and Taglish where natural.
- **Trust signals**: "Japan-based", "authentic", "ships nationwide", real photos with our watermark.
- Avoid: aggressive sales talk, all caps, too many accent colors.

## 6. Messaging building blocks

- **One-liner:** "Japan-based anime plushies, figures & goods — shipped anywhere in the Philippines."
- **Hashtags:** `#PlushAlley` `#AnimePlushPH` `#AnimeMerchPH` `#TaguigShop`
- **CTA examples:** "Adopt yours today 🐾", "Message us to reserve", "Check our pinned post for more details!"
