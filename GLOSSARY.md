# Glossary — Plush Alley landing

Landing-page terms only. Inventory terms (Product, Variant, Central Stock) live in the inventory repo's `GLOSSARY.md`.

**Channel** — A place where customers can buy from Plush Alley: the website, Shopee, Lazada, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or in person at events.

**Review** — A buyer's rating and comment left on a marketplace Channel (currently Shopee) after a completed order. Shown as written, with the reviewer's marketplace username exactly as the marketplace displays it (masked names stay masked).

**Event pickup** — Collecting a paid or reserved order in person at a Plush Alley event. Events are announced on Facebook.
